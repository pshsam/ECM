Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy explains what information ECM (ecm-coupon.com, "the site") handles and how.

1. Information we collect

You can use the site without an account, and we don't ask for information that identifies you. Information is collected only in these cases:

  • If you email us: your email address and message, used to reply and to check reports.
  • When you visit: access logs such as IP address, browser type and time of visit, kept by our hosting service (GitHub Pages) to run the site and keep it secure. We don't use them to identify anyone.
  • Copied codes: when you copy a code, the site remembers it in your own browser (local storage) so it can show which codes you already copied. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

2. Cookies and ads

We use Google Analytics to count visits. It uses cookies to collect the pages you view, time on page, how you arrived, device and browser type, and approximate location (city level). It does not collect your name or email, and we only see the totals. You can block cookies in your browser settings or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

The site may show third-party ads, including Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on those visits. You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of other vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

3. How long we keep information

  • Emails are deleted within one year after we finish handling them.
  • Access logs follow the hosting service's policy.
  • Google deletes Google Analytics data 14 months after collection.

4. Sharing

We don't sell or give your personal information to others, except when the law requires it. These services process data for the site under their own privacy policies: GitHub Pages (hosting), Google Analytics (visit statistics) and Google AdSense (ads).

5. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show, correct or delete messages you sent us. Email the address below and we will handle it promptly.

6. Children

ECM is a general-audience site and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

7. Links to other sites

The site links to game publishers and other websites. Their own privacy policies apply there, and ECM is not responsible for them.

8. Contact

ECM is run from South Korea. Email: contact@ecm-coupon.com

9. Changes

We may update this policy when laws or the site change. Updates appear on this page with a new effective date.
